### Added

- A Statlocker theme: graphite panels with a mint-green accent.
- A Flint Snow theme: coral and midnight navy with pink trim.
- The Statlocker and Flint Snow themes are also on the website's theme picker.
- A Void theme: OLED black with electric violet trim, also on the website.
- A DeadChaps theme: near-black panels with DeadChaps purple, also on the website.
- A Pretty in Pink theme: candy pink and cream with lilac and sky-blue touches, also on the website.
- Discord presence can show the Deadlock+ logo where Discord would show the Deadlock logo.
- A Delay setting holds back what Discord presence shows by up to two minutes, against spoilers and stream sniping.
- Turning presence off or changing its level hides it at once, without waiting out the delay.
- A "Queue stopped" alert in the game overlay tells you when your queue ends without a match while you're tabbed out.
- Turn the "Queue stopped" alert off in Settings > Notifications.
- The website comes in every language the app has, with a language picker in its footer.
- The website offers your browser's language in a small banner you can dismiss.
- Deadlock+ can now show important notices from the team in a banner at the top of the app.

#### Theme options

- Each theme's card in Settings > Appearance now holds that theme's own options.
- Turn single effects off, like Nightshift's rain or Flint Snow's tail.
- Pick an accent colour of your own for any theme but High contrast and Catppuccin.
- A custom accent keeps text readable by shifting its lightness only as far as contrast needs.
- Themes based on something link to it, like Deadlock API or Deadlock Night Shift on Twitch.

#### Theme effects

- Most themes now draw their own art and animation behind the page.
- In the Deadlock theme, dust drifts through the air and swirls when your mouse moves through it.
- In the Nightshift theme, rain runs down a fogged window that your mouse wipes clear.
- Nightshift's string lights sway over a teal night street whose windows follow your local time.
- Nightshift's gold art deco frames and eye medallion catch the light now and then.
- On Wednesday nights at Night Shift's usual show time, Nightshift lights up "Clock In" and a clock on your local time.
- In the Flint Snow theme, snow swirls around your mouse and stars join into constellations.
- In Flint Snow, your mouse leaves a trail of sparkles.
- Flint's tail lies in the snowbank and flicks now and then.
- Paw tracks cross Flint Snow's snowbank every so often.
- In the Void theme, a black hole turns in the corner, pulling in faint specks of light.
- In the DeadChaps theme, a sigil turns slowly in the corner while dust drifts past.
- In the Pretty in Pink theme, fluffy clouds drift past and little hearts float up the sides.
- Pretty in Pink ties a polka-dot bow on the corner that wiggles now and then.
- Turn theme effects off with the Theme effects switch in Settings > Appearance.
- Reduced motion keeps the still art and stops everything that moves.
- Effects pause while the Deadlock+ window is hidden or not focused.

#### Match front page

- When Deadlock+ captures a match from the post-game screen, its front page lands on the window by itself.
- If Deadlock+ is in the background then, the front page waits until you come back to the window.
- After a match, the critics' column fills in as soon as Deadlock+ has graded it.
- Turn it off with "Front page after each match" in Settings > Notifications.
- Pick which match modes get an after-match front page; bot matches and custom lobbies are left out by default.
- When a front page is waiting while you're in game, the game overlay tells you.
- Turn the front page waiting alert off with "Front page waiting" in Settings > Notifications.
- A "Front page" button in a match's details prints that match as a newspaper front page.
- A front page's headlines come from the match: comebacks, first blood, Mid Boss claims, falls and top performers.
- A front page carries a box score, lane report, objectives timeline and short briefs.
- "Open full match" on an after-match front page takes you to that match's details.
- The critics' column shows the Deadlock+ grades when the match has them.
- A weather box shows your ping when Deadlock+ recorded it for that match.
- The paper lands on the table a different way each time, and simply fades in with reduced motion.
- Save or copy the front page as an image.
- Close it with Escape, the Close button or a click on the table.

#### Deadlock+ Dispatch

- A new Dispatch page prints your Deadlock week and month as a newspaper.
- A Weekly Edition comes out every Monday and covers the week before.
- A Monthly Edition comes out on the 1st and covers the month before.
- A Collector's Copy in gold foil comes out when a ranked season ends and covers that season's ranked matches.
- A Year-End Special in black and gold comes out on December 1 and covers the twelve months before it.
- An edition needs at least three matches in what it covers.
- "This week so far" shows the current week's paper as it fills up.
- Editions lie on a coffee table: this week so far, the newest Weekly and Monthly, and any specials.
- Past weeks lie in a bundle per month; open a bundle to deal its copies out.
- Pick up a copy to read it, and put it back with Escape or the Table button.
- Headlines cover your record, hours played, top hero, best streak, longest session, rank moves and more.
- The game of the week is picked from your best matches by KDA, then by Deadlock+ grade.
- "The company you keep" names the friends you partied with most and the enemy who beat you most.
- Issued editions are kept as they were printed, so old papers never change.
- Every past edition back to your first recorded match can be printed when you want it.
- A Monthly Edition opens as a magazine; turn its pages with the arrow keys.
- Save or copy the front page or any section as an image, in 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9.
- A notification tells you when a new edition is out; it never shows in the game overlay.
- Turn that notification off with "New Dispatch edition" in Settings > Notifications.

#### Catppuccin theme

- A Catppuccin theme, in the official colours of the Catppuccin palette.
- Pick any of Catppuccin's four flavours: Latte, Frappé, Macchiato or Mocha.
- Pick any of Catppuccin's 14 accents, like Mauve, Peach or Sapphire.
- Catppuccin starts as Mocha with Mauve, Catppuccin's own defaults.
- Catppuccin Mocha is also on the website's theme picker.
- Where an accent is too faint to read as text, like most accents on Latte, text uses a darker or lighter shade of it.

### Changed

- The Nightshift theme now uses the dark teal and gold of the Deadlock Night Shift show.
- Text in every theme now meets a 4.5:1 contrast ratio, most visibly Daylight's darker gold.
- Red buttons in the Deadlock, Midnight and High contrast themes now use dark text.
- Discord shows the match clock in the Hideout by default.
- The "Time in this state" timer is now called "Time since Deadlock started", which is what it measures.
- Exported scoreboard images have a new footer with the Deadlock+ logo on the left and deadlockplus.app on the right.
- Saving settings and match data no longer freezes the app for a moment.
- Dispatch editions are kept in their own file, listed on its own row in Settings > Storage.
- The website loads about half as much on a phone and keeps its pictures and fonts cached between visits.
- Deadlock+ starts faster: Dispatch's text loads only when you open Dispatch.
- Dispatch editions open faster.
- Match details and the Builds page show up faster.
- Deadlock+ reads the game's memory in small steps at low priority, so it takes less CPU from the game.
- With reduced motion on, nothing moves on the page, including video previews and scrolling.
- The Motion setting in Deadlock+ now wins over the Windows reduced motion setting everywhere.
